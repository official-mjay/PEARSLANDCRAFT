OUR STORY

More than a brand.
A journey of faith.

Intentionally crafted, purposefully lived.

Pearls & Crafts was born from a love for faith, intentional living, creativity, organization and personal growth.

THE BEGINNING

Why Pearls & Crafts began.

I started Pearls & Crafts because I wanted to create a brand that reflects the things I genuinely value—faith, intentional living, creativity, organization, and personal growth.

I wanted to create products that could help people become more intentional about their lives, while also expressing their faith and personality.

THE INSPIRATION

It started with journaling.

Journaling has always been a way for me to pray, reflect, process my thoughts, set goals, and document different seasons of my life.

I realized journals could be more than blank pages—they could help people plan, reflect, grow spiritually, and become more intentional.

THE NAME

Why “Pearls & Crafts”?

Pearls

Something valuable, beautiful, and worth preserving.

Crafts

The intentional process of creating something meaningful.

Together, Pearls & Crafts represents the idea that our lives are valuable and should be intentionally crafted.
THE VISION

A faith-based lifestyle brand.

In the next 3–5 years, Pearls & Crafts is envisioned as a recognized faith-based stationery and lifestyle brand that helps people live intentionally—across Nigeria and internationally.

The dream includes expanding journals, planners, T-shirts and other meaningful lifestyle products, building a community around faith, purpose, growth and intentional living, and creating opportunities for other creatives.
